# Animal Facts Ep. 1: Why is this dad pregnant? One narration line per line, as approved in the Script.
Why is this dad... pregnant?
Meet the seahorse.
In seahorse families, the dads carry the babies.
Mom drops her eggs into a pouch on his belly... then swims away.
Now he's pregnant, and seahorses are already one of the slowest fish on Earth.
With no stomach, he snacks all day long.
Every morning, mom visits, and they dance together.
A few weeks later, he gives birth. Not to one baby... to up to two thousand.
And sometimes, the very next day... he's pregnant again.
So this Father's Day... thank a seahorse.
